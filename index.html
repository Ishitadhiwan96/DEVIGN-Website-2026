OUR STORY
GBPIET Chapter Est. 2024

FOUNDED AT GBPIET TO EXPLORE TECHNOLOGY, CREATIVITY, & INNOVATION.

Founded at Govind Ballabh Pant Institute of Engineering & Technology (GBPIET) in 2024 by Yashpreet Singh, DEVIGN was created to give students a dedicated space to explore technology, creativity, and innovation together.

Devign brings together designers, engineers, creative storytellers, and logistics coordinators under one community. Rather than treating design and software as isolated academic exercises, DEVIGN provides a real-world testing ground where students build platforms that serve the campus and its signature events.
